03Boilerplate

Approved short and long descriptions for use in press materials.

Short (50 words)

Blue Pulse Studios is an independent, award-winning studio telling bold, unflinching stories in film, television, and video games. Headquartered in Los Angeles and Pittsburgh, the studio champions the fearless, the visionary, and the unhinged — crafting dark narratives that refuse to play it safe.

Long (110 words)

Blue Pulse Studios is an independent production company working in horror and genre cinema, prestige television, and interactive video games. Founded in 2024 and headquartered in Los Angeles and Pittsburgh, the studio operates as a boutique — small rooms, patient timelines, in-camera craft — with a debut feature, Stay Home, in production for 2027 and a back catalog of award-winning short films including The Painting (2025). The studio also publishes the tabletop wargame system Red Winter through its imprint Kriegsspiel Press. Blue Pulse believes restraint is a feature, not a bug, and that the best horror leaves the room slowly.
