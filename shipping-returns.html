Production & ship times

In-stock items ship within 3–5 business days. Limited and made-to-order pieces ship when their production run completes — the estimated window is listed on each product page, and we email you when your piece leaves the studio.

Shipping

We ship from the United States. Rates are calculated at checkout by weight and destination. International customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.

Returns

Unworn, unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Numbered limited editions and signed pieces are final sale unless they arrive damaged.

Damaged or lost orders

If something arrives damaged, email us a photo within 7 days and we’ll replace it or refund you — whichever you prefer. If tracking stalls, contact us and we’ll chase the carrier so you don’t have to.

Contact

All shipping questions: production@bluepulsestudios.com.
