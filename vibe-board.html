04Typography
Something is breathing down there.
Fraunces · 300 light · -0.025em · italics carry the glow

Display is Fraunces — a serif with soft pressure, set light and tight. Italics are reserved for the words we want the reader to hear out loud. Body and interface text is Inter, tracked wide and set small, like the label on a specimen jar.

05Motion

Everything moves like it’s underwater. Nothing snaps. Nothing bounces.

Drift
