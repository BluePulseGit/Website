Moth Country

Past the last streetlight, the woods change — and something enormous has been waiting a long time for company.

Animated Series Television Horror-Fantasy
01The Series

A coming-of-age descent into the country of the dark.

When three kids follow a light into the forest behind their town, they cross into Moth Country — a violet, nocturnal realm ruled by the Moth, an ancient guardian the size of a house, and the smaller, stranger creatures that keep its secrets.

What starts as a dare becomes a descent. Moth Country remembers every child who ever wandered in, and lets very few wander back out. Lantern-light and long shadows, wonder and teeth — it is equal parts folk nightmare and the kind of friendship you only make in the dark.

An animated horror-fantasy series about the pull of the woods, the friends who follow you into them, and everything that watches from just past the treeline.
