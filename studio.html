Manifesto · 2026

Blue Pulse Studios is an independent, award-winning studio hell-bent on telling bold, unflinching stories in film, television, and video games. We champion the fearless, the visionary, and the unhinged — crafting dark narratives that refuse to play it safe.

With headquarters in Los Angeles and Pittsburgh, we’re here to shake things up, make people feel something, and craft stories that stay with audiences well after the credits roll.

01What we make

Three mediums, one temperament. The story decides the form.

I · Film

Feature cinema

Boutique-scale, auteur-driven horror and genre features. Shot in-camera when we can. Released to be seen in a dark room with other people.

II · Television

Long-form dread

Limited series and anthologies for the nights that need a slow unravelling. Built for streaming, written for patience.

III · Video Games

Interactive horror

Playable stories where the camera is the player. First-person atmosphere, branching narrative, sound design that breathes.

What we champion

Three things keep us awake at night.

i.

The fearless

Stories that refuse to soften their edges. Writers and directors willing to sit in discomfort long enough to find something true inside it.

ii.

The visionary

A singular voice, handled with care. We back the weird idea, the unproven hand, the film that shouldn’t work — and then does.

iii.

The unhinged

The stuff that keeps you up. Dark narratives that play for keeps. If it makes the room uncomfortable, we’re probably interested.
