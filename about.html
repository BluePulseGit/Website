02What we stand for

Our mission.

We exist to champion original stories. Every legacy franchise was once a risky, bold idea that someone fearlessly backed — and the industry has grown allergic to that risk. We take it on purpose: developing and producing original films, television, animation, and interactive entertainment with the highest creative quality, so that new worlds get made instead of recycled.

Our vision.

Over the next decade we’re building a next-generation studio that empowers creators to tell their stories — a home where filmmakers, animators, writers and game designers can develop original IP across formats without losing ownership of the voice that made it worth telling. Not another production company. A place where a first idea can become a franchise.

Our values.

The production values we hold ourselves to on every project, in every room.
